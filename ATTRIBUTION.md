# Name and attribution

## Public names

- product and project: **DubClean**;
- portable distribution: **DubClean Portable**;
- original speech-cleaning model: **DubClean Voice**;
- original music-and-effects model: **DubClean M&E**.

The name does not depend on a website or domain. The repository or model page
from which a copy was obtained is the appropriate source and contact point for
that copy.

## Recommended audio-track label

When publishing a track processed with DubClean, we strongly recommend:

```text
<name of the dub or translation> · cleaned with DubClean
```

Russian-language form:

```text
<название озвучки или перевода> · очищено с помощью DubClean
```

Compact form:

```text
<name of the dub or translation> · DubClean
```

Examples:

```text
Студийный дубляж · cleaned with DubClean
Многоголосый перевод · очищено с помощью DubClean
Авторский перевод · DubClean
```

`cleaned with DubClean` is preferred to `by DubClean`: it makes clear that
DubClean cleaned the audio and did not create, own or endorse the translation.

This output-label recommendation is voluntary and is not an additional
condition of the AGPL. It may be used factually to identify an unmodified or
modified DubClean pipeline, provided it does not imply sponsorship,
endorsement or authorship of the underlying translation.

Source distributions and user interfaces must still preserve the copyright
and license notices required by the applicable licenses.
